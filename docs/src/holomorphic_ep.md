# Holomorphic EP

Holomorphic equilibrium propagation replaces a small real finite difference with a
finite-radius contour in the complex nudging plane. If the total energy ``F_\beta`` and its
equilibrium branch are holomorphic inside that contour, the exact objective gradient
is the first Fourier coefficient of the phase-wise parameter derivative.

```julia
using ADTypes: AutoForwardDiff
using EquilibriumPropagation

model = EPModel(
    energy=(state, parameters, input, model_state) ->
        sum(state .^ 2) / 2 - sum(parameters .* state),
    cost=(state, target, parameters, model_state) ->
        sum((state .- target) .^ 2) / 2,
    readout=(state, parameters, model_state) -> state,
    initial_state=(parameters, input, model_state) -> zeros(length(parameters)),
)
problem = EPProblem(model, [0.3, -0.2], NamedTuple(), nothing, [-0.4, 0.7])
solver = Relaxation(dt=0.5, maxiters=300, abstol=1e-12)

algorithm = EPAlgorithm(
    HolomorphicEP(0.2; points=16),
    solver;
    state_ad=AutoForwardDiff(),
    parameter_ad=AutoForwardDiff(),
)
gradient, stats = ep_gradient(problem, algorithm)
```

For radius `r` and `N` points, the protocol solves equilibria at

```math
\beta_k = r\exp(2\pi\mathrm{i}k/N), \qquad k=0,\ldots,N-1,
```

and estimates

```math
\nabla_\theta\mathcal L =
\frac{1}{Nr}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}
\partial_\theta F_{\beta_k}(s^*_{\beta_k};\theta)
\exp(-2\pi\mathrm{i}k/N).
```

`N=2` reduces to symmetric EP. Increasing `N` suppresses Fourier alias terms while
retaining a finite teaching radius. The radius must remain inside a region where the
chosen equilibrium exists and varies holomorphically; a larger radius is not always
more stable.

## Complex-valued model contract

The nudged state is complex. Functions acting on active state values must therefore
be holomorphic. In particular:

- use `x^2` rather than `abs2(x)`;
- use `transpose(A)` rather than the conjugating adjoint `A'`;
- do not use `abs`, `conj`, `real`, clipping, ReLU, or comparisons of active values;
- choose a holomorphic activation such as `tanh` or sigmoid, while accounting for its
  complex singularities; and
- ensure every sampled complex phase converges.

`QuadraticPotential`, `BipolarSquaredError`, and the recurrent coupling generated by
`ContinuousHopfield` follow these rules. Custom potentials, activations, and costs
remain the caller's responsibility.

Parameters are treated as real trainable values. `HolomorphicEPStats` reports
`imaginary_leakage`, the norm of the imaginary part discarded after Fourier
extraction. Significant leakage usually indicates incomplete relaxation, inadequate
sampling, an unstable contour, or a non-holomorphic model.

The formulation follows Laborieux and Zenke, [*Holomorphic Equilibrium Propagation
Computes Exact Gradients Through Finite Size
Oscillations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00530), NeurIPS 2022.
